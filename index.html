Are People Too Superstitious to Have Babies on the 13th?

The number 13 is considered to be an unlucky number under common superstition. But just how significant might the impact of this superstition be? Let's analyze US birth data from 1994-2014 to see how dates falling on the 13th correlate to birth trends. Use the buttons at the top-right to navigate!

1: The Least Popular Birthdays

Dates that fall on the 25th, 31st, and 24th appear to have the lowest number of births on average. This is followed by the 13th - note that unlike the other dates experiencing fewer births on average, this date does not occur around a major American holiday!

2: Birth Trends for Dates falling on the 13th vs Not

Fewer births seem to occur on the 13th of the month, but has this always been the case?

Birth trends between 1994-2014 show that dates falling on the 13th of the month generally have fewer births on average than dates that do not fall on the 13th, but notice that there are some exceptions! Hover over the chart to get a better idea of where these occur.

3: Birth Trends for Dates falling on the 13th by Day of Week

Friday the 13th holds an especially significant place in superstition, but is this day really that unpopular?

When comparing birth trends for dates falling on the 13th across different days of the week, we find that weekends tend to have the fewest births on average. This may not have much to do with the 13th - hospital wards have reduced staff availability on weekends. But it does seem that between 1994-2014, Friday the 13th has had relatively fewer births on average than other weekdays falling on the 13th, with the exception of Monday.
